# Treatment: *Whitechapel, 1888* (Artistic True Crime — Woodcut Print)

**Style:** Woodcut Print (`woodcut`)  
**Format:** 1920×1080, 24 fps, 45.0 s  
**Language:** US English (`en-US-AndrewNeural`), letterpress captions in the bottom paper margin (`IM Fell English`), no modern UI boxes.  
**Color Logic:** Near-black ink (`#141311`), warm laid-paper (`#f2ebd9`), and **one colour plate**: deep oxblood (`#9e1c1c`) reserved strictly for the gaslight flame / blood trace / the *"From Hell"* wax seal.

## 1. Three Candidate Structures (and Choice)
1. **Suspect Lineup:** Five portraits carved in sequence. *Weakness:* static, misses the London fog atmosphere.
2. **Map of the Five Murders:** Overhead street plan carved out. *Weakness:* too analytical, loses the woodcut-novel emotional dread.
3. **Five Plates of an Autumn Night (CHOSEN):** Inspired by Masereel/Ward wordless woodcut novels—5 self-contained carved plates separated by real paper-peel / new-impression transitions:
   - **Plate 1 (0.0–9.0 s):** *The Fog of Whitechapel* — Gaslight lantern carved radially into black wood, cobbled alley, terraced rooftops, title carved into the sky block (`WHITECHAPEL · 1888`).
   - **Plate 2 (9.0–18.5 s):** *The Footsteps* — Low angle on wet cobblestones and fog; silhouettes of a top-hatted figure vanishing into an archway as oxblood light bleeds from the carved lantern.
   - **Plate 3 (18.5–28.0 s):** *The Five Dates* — Five vertical carved columns/tallies cut stroke by stroke into the dark block (`AUG 31` · `SEP 8` · `SEP 30` · `SEP 30` · `NOV 9`), each tally glowing faint oxblood on the cut.
   - **Plate 4 (28.0–37.0 s):** *The "From Hell" Letter* — Mirrored woodblock carving of `"FROM HELL"` that flips/peels into true print with a single oxblood wax seal.
   - **Plate 5 (37.0–45.0 s):** *The Unsolved Corner* — Return to the empty cobbled alley at dawn; the gaslight goes out, leaving an uncarved black corner where the killer vanished.

## 2. Sound Design (Woodcut Palette)
- **Score:** Contrabass/cello low D drone, sparse pizzicato, frame drum on plate impressions, and a 2-second **digital silence** before Plate 4 (*"From Hell"*).
- **Foley:** Knife-into-wood gouge scrapes synced to stroke reveals, heavy block thump + paper peel on transitions, distant foghorn/wind, footsteps on cobbles.
