## 2. YouTube Description (SEO-Optimized + Chapters + CC BY 4.0 Credits)

At 8:05 PM on October 29, $6.3 billion began pouring out of Binance across 10 blockchains at once. Twenty minutes later, the vaults were empty—and not a single blockchain was broken or private key leaked.

Every transaction had been legitimately signed by Binance’s own internal system.

Five months earlier, a single poisoned developer utility—just **70 bytes** larger than the original (`3,344` bytes swapped for `3,414` bytes)—slipped past endpoint security during a fake startup coding interview. From there, attackers linked three engineers' laptops through an internal AI tool, launched a container, and convinced an automated teller to pre-sign **50 blank permission slips** on Ethereum.

In 2026 alone, Binance stopped **57** similar attacks. It only took one 70-byte file to make it through.

### Key Timeline & Verified Figures
- **Drain Speed:** `$6.3 billion` drained in `20 minutes` across `10 blockchains` (`167,000+ ETH` + `$1.6B USDT` swept in `3m 24s` alone)
- **The 70-Byte Swap:** `3,344-byte` developer tool silently replaced by a `3,414-byte` lookalike during a fake Round 2 coding test
- **Gas Seed:** Three `0.1 ETH` mixer withdrawals (`0.30 ETH` total) funding the fresh attacker wallet `36 hours` before the breach
- **The Freeze Race:** Tether, Circle, Ripple, and Arbitrum froze `~$2.7B–$3B` in stablecoins; attackers swapped `$60M USDT` in DeFi before Binance severed access at `12:10 AM`
- **Unfreezable Haul:** Hackers retained `420,000 ETH` and `18,200 BTC` (`~$2.4 billion` in raw unfreezable crypto)
- **Bailout & Attribution:** `$4.6B` paid back in 24 hours; `$6.3B` hole plugged on November 12 from SAFU emergency funds & corporate treasury; U.S. DOJ & FBI obtained a court order seizing `$1.2B` and attributed the heist to North Korea's **TraderTraitor** unit (~4× larger than Bybit)
- **The Open Questions:** `57` other engineering lures blocked in 2026, yet Binance still hasn't disclosed which internal AI tool bridged three laptops or how one container triggered `50` blank checks with zero rate limits or human sign-off.
